Eula

Passives:

> Dance of Aphros:
Eula starts the game with a glacial seal, the Lawrence Clan's family crest. Whenever she wins with a card that she hasn't played yet, it gains one stack. Once it
reaches four stacks, it transforms and she creates a 'Glacial Illumination' card in her hand. Once she plays 'Glacial Illumination', it transforms back and resets its stacks.

  ~ A legacy of tyranny reforged into true 'noble obligation'.


Cards:

> Ocean Swell Step[F][6]:
 Eula performs an upwards slash, dealing 1 damage.

    ~ No evildoers shall escape this rising tide.  


> Flutter of Frost[F][3]:
With an elegant whirlwind of strikes emboldened by cryo, she deals 2 damage. This deals +1 damage if she possesses any glacial seal stacks. 

    ~ To be free and unbound like the ocean spray-yes, that would be all one needs.


> Icetide Tempo[F][2]:
With the poise and grace of a dancer, Eula deals 1 damage and builds momentum, gaining a damage bonus that decays from +2 to +1 over two rounds. 

    ~ The rise and fall of her greatsword mirror the crash of icy waves. 


> Roiling Rime[S][3]:
Brandishing her great sword, Eula creates a fierce burst of cryo and frost. This card gets +4 power if she has 3 or more glacial seal stacks. It deals 2 damage plus +1 damage for every 2 missing hp her opponent has lost from their starting hp. A maximum of 4 total bonus damage can be added this way. 

    ~ 


> Vengeance, Severed with Ice[M][6]:
 Eula creates two ice swords that cleave her opponent, dealing 1 damage each and giving them -2 power for one round. Damage modifiers apply to each instance of damage separately. 

    ~ "Let this grievance be considered settled." 


> [Ultimate] Glacial Illumination[M][7]:
Eula transforms her glacial seal into a lightfall sigil which violently explodes for 6 damage. 

    ~ So the waves roiled, swallowing the crown of the nobles. And ever after did each drop amidst the icy waves reflect a golden crown, each and every droplet of the spray shining with its light.
